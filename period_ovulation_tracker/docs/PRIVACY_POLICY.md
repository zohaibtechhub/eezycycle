# EezyCycle Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **EezyCycle** (“we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the EezyCycle mobile application and related services (the “App”).

EezyCycle is a personal wellness tool for period tracking, ovulation and fertility estimates, pregnancy-mode insights, education, and optional partner or community features. It is **not** a medical device and does **not** diagnose, treat, or prevent disease. Predictions are estimates. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical decisions.

For privacy requests, contact: **info@eezycycle.com**

---

## 1. Who we are

EezyCycle provides reproductive and cycle-related tracking and educational content. This policy applies to guest users and registered accounts.

If you do not agree with this policy, do not use the App or provide health information.

---

## 2. Information we collect

We collect only what is needed to run App features you choose to use.

### 2.1 Account and profile

- Email address and password (registered accounts), or Google Sign-In identifiers
- Display name and profile photo (if you add them)
- Birth year (age gating and personalization)
- Height, weight, and similar onboarding answers (if you provide them)
- Device identifiers used to keep your session (including guest sessions)

### 2.2 Health and fitness information

Depending on what you log, this may include:

- Period start/end dates, cycle length, and related calendar data
- Symptoms, moods, notes, and activity chips (e.g. walking, yoga)
- Ovulation or pregnancy test results you enter
- Sexual activity logs and related private notes
- Pregnancy mode details (e.g. due date) and pregnancy-day insights preferences
- Goals and experience mode (e.g. track cycle, trying to conceive, pregnancy)
- Onboarding health answers (e.g. birth control, PCOS-related answers) when provided

### 2.3 App activity and community content

- App preferences (notifications, themes, quiet hours)
- Secret Chat posts, comments, tags, and images you upload (visible to other users in the community)
- Support messages you send to us

### 2.4 Device and push information

- Device or installation identifiers
- Push subscription / player IDs (e.g. OneSignal) and notification preferences
- Basic technical data needed for security and delivery (e.g. app version, timezone for reminders)

We do **not** sell your personal or reproductive health data to advertisers.

## 3. Why we use your information

| Purpose | Examples |
|--------|----------|

| **Personalization** | Phase-based insights, articles, pregnancy daily content |

## 4. Guest vs registered account

- **Guest:** You can use core tracking with a device-linked session after accepting this Privacy Policy and health-data processing consent. Guest data is stored to provide the App; it is tied to your device session until you upgrade or delete it.
- **Registered account:** Email/password or Google Sign-In lets you recover access, link a partner, and keep data across devices. Upgrading a guest merges eligible data into your account under the same privacy rules.

Consent to the Privacy Policy and to processing of personal health data is required before health features sync to our servers.

---

## 5. Partner sharing (opt-in)

Partner linking is **optional**. When you connect a partner with a Partner Code:

**Your partner may see**

- High-level cycle phase and predicted fertile / period days
- Limited profile information you choose to share (e.g. photo)
- Relationship-oriented tips intended for partners

**Your partner cannot see**

- Sex logs
- Detailed symptoms
- Test results
- Private notes and other intimate fields reserved to your account

You can disconnect partner access at any time in the App. Disconnecting stops new sharing; ask support if you need help confirming unlink status.

---

## 6. Secret Chat (community)

Secret Chat is a **community** surface. Posts and comments you publish (and images you attach) can be seen by other App users. Do not post information you are not comfortable sharing publicly within the App.

Content may remain visible until you delete it (where the App allows) or until we remove it for policy or safety reasons. Deleting your account may anonymize or remove associated community content as described in Section 10.

---

## 7. Third parties and service providers

We use processors that help us operate the App. They may process limited data on our instructions:

| Provider / category | Role |
|---------------------|------|
| Hosting / database | Store account and health records securely |
| Google Sign-In | Authentication when you choose Google |
| Email (SMTP) | Password reset and transactional mail |
| OneSignal / FCM / APNs | Push notification delivery |
| Device OS services | Local notifications, timezone, storage |

These parties are not authorized to sell your health data. Push providers receive device/subscription identifiers and notification content needed to deliver reminders — we avoid putting intimate health details in push bodies where possible.

---

## 8. Encryption and security

- Data in transit uses **HTTPS / TLS**.
- Account passwords are stored as secure hashes (not plaintext).
- Health and account records are stored on secured servers; intimate fields are protected with access controls and, where implemented, encryption at rest.
- Access by staff is minimized and intended only for operating, securing, or legally required support of the service — not casual browsing of intimate logs.

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. Please protect your device and partner codes.

---

## 9. Retention

We keep your information while your guest session or account is active and as needed to provide the App.

- Active accounts / guests: retained to deliver tracking, sync, and reminders.
- Push scheduling records and device subscription IDs: retained while notifications are enabled and removed or invalidated when you disable push, log out, or delete your account (subject to short technical delay).
- Inactive guests with no registered account may be deleted after a prolonged inactivity period (for example, up to **24 months**), or sooner if you request deletion.
- Backups and security logs may persist for a limited time after deletion, then are purged on a rolling schedule.

---

## 10. Your choices: access, export, and deletion

Subject to applicable law, you may:

- **Access / correct** profile and health logs in the App
- **Export** your data (where available in Settings, or by contacting us)
- **Delete** your account and associated health data via in-App controls (when available) or by emailing **info@eezycycle.com**
- **Revoke** partner sharing and notification preferences in the App
- **Withdraw consent** for health processing by deleting your data and stopping use of the App

Deletion requests: we will delete or anonymize personal and health records tied to your account, cancel scheduled push jobs where applicable, and handle community content by deletion or anonymization. Some residual data may remain briefly in backups.

If you believe a minor has provided data in violation of this policy, contact us and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

Cycle, ovulation, fertile-window, and pregnancy-related estimates can be inaccurate. Decisions about contraception, conception, pregnancy, or medical treatment should be made with a qualified clinician.
